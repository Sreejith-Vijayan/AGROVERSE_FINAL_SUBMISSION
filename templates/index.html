{{ leaf(28) }} Static Soil Properties

Provisional regional soil texture and chemical attributes for Palakkad Farm.

PROVISIONAL DATASET
Provisional Dataset Notice: These values represent provisional regional survey estimates and require on-ground laboratory core sampling verification. They are strictly not confirmed farm-level core measurements. Unverified soil attributes (nitrogen, sand, silt) are excluded from this platform.
Soil Resilience Management Notes

The medium-loamy alluvium of the Chittur-Kollengode paddy corridor exhibits moderate sub-surface clay content (~28.4%) enabling sound water retention during the Southwest Monsoon. However, low soil organic carbon (0.78%) makes the soil prone to crusting and rapid drying under summer heat stress (temperatures ≥ 35 °C). Incorporating legume green manure (Sesbania/Dhaincha) in crop rotations significantly enhances topsoil organic matter.

Scientific Observation: Volumetric soil moisture peaks in the Southwest Monsoon months (July–August: ~0.38–0.42 m³/m³) and drops in the dry summer months (February–April: ~0.14–0.18 m³/m³). The relationship between precipitation and SMAP satellite soil moisture is purely descriptive; correlation does not imply direct causation.
