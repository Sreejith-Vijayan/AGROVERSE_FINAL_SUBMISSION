AGROVERSE Agriculture CSV package
Geographic scope: Palakkad, Kerala (as requested in the conversation).

Files:
- crop_profiles.csv: crop catalogue and qualitative profiles
- crop_calendar.csv: season/calendar rows; date fields intentionally blank pending local verification
- crop_climate_sensitivity.csv: qualitative crop-climate relationships
- crop_water_requirements.csv: qualitative categories; numeric values intentionally blank
- recommendation_rules.csv: transparent conditional recommendation rules
- source_register.csv: institutional source starting points

Data-quality notes:
1. This is an initial hackathon knowledge-base package, not a validated agronomic advisory dataset.
2. Qualitative sensitivity/category labels are preliminary and must not be treated as calibrated numeric scores.
3. Blank numeric/date fields mean not yet verified; they do not mean zero or not applicable.
4. Source register entries are institutional landing pages. Retrieve and cite the exact publication/table/page before using numerical claims.
5. Verify Palakkad-specific calendars and crop-area rankings before presenting them as district facts.
6. Satellite soil-moisture and vegetation observations must be interpreted at their native spatial/temporal resolution.
